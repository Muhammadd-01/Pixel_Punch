# PixelPunch — Zones and Fictional Villains

## 1. Playable Zones

### Zone 1: NexoVate Office District
- **Theme**: Stylized corporate cartoon office with cubicles, desks, whiteboards, and a server room.
- **Locations**:
  - *Reception*: Spawn area with cozy couches and welcome desk.
  - *Open Office*: Cluster of wooden desks and computers.
  - *Meeting Room*: Glass-walled whiteboard strategy room.
  - *Creative Dept*: Vibrant corner with design drafts.
  - *Server Room*: Dark arcade-themed challenge zone with glowing server racks.
- **Villains**: Bug Phantom, Captain Deadline.
- **Unlock Status**: Unlocked by default.

### Zone 2: Neon Battle District
- **Theme**: Futuristic training sector with glowing floors and holographic barriers.
- **Locations**:
  - *Neon Gateway*: Entrance portal.
  - *Training Arena*: Central combat platform.
  - *Speed Track*: High-velocity lane.
  - *Glow Platform*: Elevated obstacle sector.
- **Villains**: Firewall Guardian, Lag Lord.
- **Unlock Status**: Requires completing Zone 1.

### Zone 3: Pixel Rooftop
- **Theme**: High-altitude rooftop overlooking a sunset cartoon skyline.
- **Locations**:
  - *Stairway Exit*: Entry landing.
  - *Vent Corridor*: Narrow platform flanked by ventilation ducts.
  - *Skyline Lookout*: Scenic railed viewing area.
  - *Boss Arena*: Circular combat stage.
- **Villains**: Dark Designer, Chaos Manager.
- **Unlock Status**: Requires completing Zone 2.

## 2. Fictional Workplace Villains

> **Disclaimer**: All villains are completely fictional computer-controlled game characters inspired by workplace humor. None represent real individuals.

### 1. The Bug Phantom
- **Archetype**: Trickster
- **Theme**: Coding bugs, edge cases, and unexpected glitches.
- **Stats**: 80 HP, 170 Speed, 10 Attack, 5 Defense.
- **Special**: Evasive Burst (Teleportation).
- **Signature Taunt**: *"That was not a bug, that was a FEATURE!"*

### 2. Captain Deadline
- **Archetype**: Heavy Hitter
- **Theme**: Time pressure and project delivery crunch.
- **Stats**: 120 HP, 90 Speed, 22 Attack, 15 Defense.
- **Special**: Ground Slam (Area Quake).
- **Signature Taunt**: *"Your time is up! Consider this your FINAL review!"*

### 3. Firewall Guardian
- **Archetype**: Tank
- **Theme**: IT security, blocked ports, and access restriction.
- **Stats**: 160 HP, 80 Speed, 14 Attack, 25 Defense.
- **Special**: Protective Shield (Damage Absorption).
- **Signature Taunt**: *"Access denied. Try getting through this!"*

### 4. Lag Lord
- **Archetype**: Speedster
- **Theme**: Network latency and high packet drop rate.
- **Stats**: 70 HP, 230 Speed, 9 Attack, 4 Defense.
- **Special**: Rapid Dash (Speed Strike).
- **Signature Taunt**: *"Catch me if your frame rate can!"*

### 5. Dark Designer
- **Archetype**: Balanced
- **Theme**: Bad UI/UX patterns and confusing interfaces.
- **Stats**: 100 HP, 140 Speed, 16 Attack, 12 Defense.
- **Special**: Charged Strike (Power Hit).
- **Signature Taunt**: *"That was not in the design specification!"*

### 6. Chaos Manager
- **Archetype**: Support / Controller
- **Theme**: Scope creep and endless alignment meetings.
- **Stats**: 90 HP, 120 Speed, 8 Attack, 10 Defense.
- **Special**: Team Recovery (Allied Healing).
- **Signature Taunt**: *"Fine! You win this sprint. The next one is mine!"*
